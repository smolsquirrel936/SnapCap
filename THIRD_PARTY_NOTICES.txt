Qt is NOT statically linked and NOT modified. Every Qt DLL and plugin sits
beside SnapCap.exe in the portable distribution and can be replaced with a copy,
which is how SnapCap satisfies the relinking freedom LGPLv3 section 4 exists to
guarantee. This is also why SnapCap ships as a portable ZIP rather than an

FFmpeg  -  LGPL v3 or later  -  DYNAMICALLY LINKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

avcodec-62.dll, avdevice-62.dll, avfilter-11.dll, avformat-62.dll, avutil-60.dll,
swresample-6.dll and swscale-9.dll.

Copyright (c) 2000-2026 the FFmpeg developers.

FFmpeg powers SnapCap's screen recording and the preview player. It is licensed
under the GNU Lesser General Public License; because this build is configured with
--enable-version3, the applicable terms are LGPL version 3 or later. A copy is in
LICENSE.LGPLv3.txt beside this file.

These libraries are DYNAMIC and replaceable: SnapCap links them at run time from
this folder, so they may be substituted with a compatible build by replacing the
DLLs, as the LGPL requires.

This is NOT a general-purpose FFmpeg. It is built by tools/build_ffmpeg.ps1 with
--disable-everything plus only the components SnapCap calls, from FFmpeg at commit
8c9502e9b048e21e1cae96477e338ac0635645ba (n8.1.2-31-g8c9502e9b0). The complete
Corresponding Source -- that FFmpeg source, the build script, and the exact
configure line -- is published alongside the SnapCap download; see Preferences ->
About -> "FFmpeg source code".

The following libraries are statically linked into the FFmpeg DLLs above. All are
permissive and none imposes a source-offer requirement; their copyright notices
and full license texts are reproduced in Appendix A:

  * libopenh264 (Cisco)          -  BSD-2-Clause  -  H.264 encoding
  * kvazaar (Tampere University) -  BSD-3-Clause  -  H.265/HEVC encoding
  * dav1d (VideoLAN)             -  BSD-2-Clause  -  AV1 decoding (recording preview;
                                    FFmpeg's own AV1 decoder has no software path)
  * SVT-AV1 (Alliance for Open Media / Intel)
                                 -  BSD-3-Clause-Clear + Alliance for Open Media
                                    Patent License 1.0  -  AV1 encoding
  * libvpx (Google / WebM)       -  BSD-3-Clause + Google patent grant
                                    -  VP9 encoding
  * Opus (Xiph.Org)              -  BSD-3-Clause  -  Opus audio encoding
  * libvpl (Intel)               -  MIT           -  Intel Quick Sync dispatcher

NVIDIA NVENC and AMD AMF are used through header-only SDKs (nv-codec-headers and
the AMF headers, both MIT, texts in Appendix A); no NVIDIA or AMD library is
linked or redistributed. The encoders are provided by the installed graphics
driver at run time.

documents mentioned them: QuaZip, hoedown and OpenSSL are not used and are not

B -- although SnapCap never calls it.)
